## Context

Support agents were juggling six tools and a sprawling knowledge base. The obvious move — a chatbot for customers — had failed twice before.

## The decision

We kept a human in the loop. The copilot drafted answers with citations directly in the reply box, and agents chose what to send.

## How we measured

We built a 600-ticket golden set with senior agents, scored for groundedness, tone and resolution. Every model or prompt change ran against it before reaching production.

## What shipped

- Inline drafts with source citations
- One-click "why this answer" panel
- Weekly quality review loop with team leads
