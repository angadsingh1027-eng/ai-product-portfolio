## Context
91.5% of US fleets operate six trucks or fewer, with operating ratios above 95% and 16.7% empty miles. Load bidding is the largest profit leak for small operators, and over $500M ARR of tooling whitespace goes unaddressed.

## Users and pain points
- **Owner-operators (about 475K in the US):** drive and dispatch at once, so bidding on the road is a safety risk, and they compete with brokers who have dedicated staff.
- **Small fleet managers (under 15 trucks):** wear every hat; cognitive overload in multi-broker negotiation hurts bid win rates and utilization.
- **Validated with 3 interviews and recordings:** "Make it simpler." Fleet utilization runs at 10-15%, and end-to-end automation from rate confirmation to dispatch to invoice is the unmet need.

## Solution
An agentic AI co-pilot that consolidates load board data, fleet context and 8+ decision variables into a ranked, profit-first view.
- **Find My Loads (MVP):** ranks loads by profit per mile, rate and urgency, with broker vetting and factoring alignment.
- **Find My Backhaul:** round-trip optimization to minimize deadhead.
- **Ask Anything:** natural-language search with preference and radius filtering, for example "Find me a flatbed Toronto to Chicago next Tuesday".
